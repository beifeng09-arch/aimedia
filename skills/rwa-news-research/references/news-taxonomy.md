# News Taxonomy

Classify RWA items into these buckets:

- `机构推进`
  - banks, asset managers, custodians, regulated financial infrastructure, institutional pilots
- `监管动态`
  - policy comments, enforcement posture, compliance guidance, regulatory framing
- `项目进展`
  - protocol launches, asset expansion, partnerships, distribution, product updates
- `基础设施变化`
  - custody, compliance rails, settlement, identity, tokenization middleware, interoperability
- `市场炒作 / 叙事噪音`
  - price-led hype, vague announcements, copycat headlines, weakly sourced narrative claims

If an item spans multiple buckets, classify by the primary reason it matters for the weekly RWA thesis.
